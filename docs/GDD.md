# Figutron — Game Design Document

## 1. Vision
**Figutron** is a robot-evolution rogue-lite inspired by *The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth* and *Brotato*. You play as a unique robot landing on a hostile alien planet, navigating procedural caves while evolving your physical form based on your actions.

## 2. Core Mechanics
### Evolution & "Snap-on" System
Unlike traditional rogue-lites where items are just stats, items in Figutron physically attach to your robot (like action figure parts).
- **Visual Silhouette:** Your body shape changes (e.g., getting bulkier, taller, or sprouting extra limbs).

### Moral Alignment: Spare vs. Neutralize
Inspired by *Undertale*, every encounter offers a choice:
- "Neutralize (Lethal):" Standard combat. Grants XP and Scrap metal. High XP leads to permanent stat upgrades.
- "Spare (Non-Lethal):" Pacify enemies. Spared enemies can become **Followers** or **Modular Upgrades** that snap onto Figutron, providing passive buffs and utility.

## 3. Story & Setting
- **The Landing:** A colonization mission goes wrong. Robot pods are scattered across a frozen alien world.
- **The Protagonist:** A "malfunctioning" unit that has gained independence from the Mother AI's hive mind.
- **Biomes:** Starts in the **Dark Caverns** (high contrast, glowing tech), moving toward the **Frozen Surface**, and eventually returning to the **Mother Spaceship**.

## 4. Factions
- **The Aliens:** Native inhabitants defending their home.
- **The Robots:** Your former "Brothers" sent to colonize. They view you as a defective unit to be recycled.
- **Aggression System:** Killing one faction makes them more aggressive in later rooms, while sparing them might unlock unique items or routes.

# Figutron Bestiary — Floor 1: Dark Caverns

## Alien Faction
### Cryo-Blob
- **Archetype:** Slime
- **Behavior:** Hops toward player. Leaves a trail of frost that slows movement.
- **Lethal Reward:** Ice Shards (Ammo/Scrap).
- **Spare Reward:** "Frost Polish" upgrade (Speed boost on ice).

### Frost-Mite
- **Archetype:** Spiky Crawler
- **Behavior:** Keeps distance and fires slow ice pebbles.
- **Lethal Reward:** High XP.
- **Spare Reward:** Becomes a "Sentry" follower.

## Robot Faction
### Rusty Peeper
- **Archetype:** Flying Drone
- **Behavior:** Erratically orbits the room. Periodically "Pings" to buff nearby robots.
- **Lethal Reward:** Scrap Metal.
- **Spare Reward:** "Signal Lens" (Reveals map secrets).
